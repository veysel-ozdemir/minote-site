Why MiNote

It does one thing, and does it well.

Most note apps have grown into productivity suites. MiNote goes the other way: a quiet place for lists, reminders and the thoughts you don't want to lose.

Private

Your notes stay on your phone

Every note lives in a local database on your device. There's no account to make, and the app never connects to the internet.

Pinning

Pin what matters

Swipe a note to the right to pin it to the top. Swipe it to the left to delete it: MiNote asks first.

Order

Your order, not the clock's

Hold a note and drag it to where it belongs. MiNote keeps it there.

Markdown

Formatting, as you type it

Headings, lists, bold, italics and quotes look right as soon as you open a note.

Auto-save

Saved as you leave

Turn on auto-save, and going back saves your note. Changed your mind? Revert puts it back the way it was.

Themes

Light, dark or your phone's

Follow your phone's setting, or choose one. The switch is instant.

Layout

A grid or a list

Two columns to see more at a glance, or one for longer notes.

Languages

English, Deutsch, Türkçe

MiNote follows your phone's language, or the one you choose in Settings.

For everyone

Made for every hand and eye

VoiceOver and TalkBack, large text, big touch targets and gentle haptics, on every screen.

A look inside

Paper, ink and one highlighter.

Clear type, sharp lines and a single lime highlight for what you've chosen, in light and in dark.

Privacy

Private by design, not by policy.

MiNote has no servers and no accounts, so there's nowhere for your notes to go. The privacy policy says the same in full.
