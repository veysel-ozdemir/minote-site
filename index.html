Why MiNote?

Most note apps have grown into bloated productivity suites. MiNote goes the other way — it does one thing, and does it beautifully.

🔒

Your notes never leave your phone

Everything is stored in a local database on your device. No server, no account, no sign-in wall. Private by design, not by policy.

🎨

Light and dark, done right

A carefully designed color palette adapts to your preference with one tap, day or night.

🧩

A grid that fits how you think

Switch between a spacious single-column view and a compact two-column grid whenever you like.

✋

Reorder notes your way

Long-press and drag any note into the order that makes sense to you — not just newest-first.

🗑️

One swipe, gone

Deleting a note is a single, satisfying swipe — with a quick confirmation so nothing disappears by accident.

✍️

Markdown, without the learning curve

Write naturally and format on the fly. MiNote renders it cleanly, so your notes look as good as the ideas behind them.

🌍

Speaks your language

Available in English, German, and Turkish, with more on the way.

♾️

No limits on your thoughts

Write a two-word reminder or a page-long journal entry — MiNote never cuts you off with an arbitrary character limit.

Who it's for

  • Anyone tired of note apps that ask for a login before they'll even let you write.
  • Anyone who wants a fast, distraction-free place for fleeting thoughts, quick lists, and everyday reminders.
  • Anyone who cares where their data actually lives.

The bottom line

MiNote isn't trying to be everything. It's trying to be the note app you actually open — because it's fast, it's yours, and it just works.
